# Calculus and derivatives

A derivative describes the local rate of change. For a differentiable scalar function, the derivative is the slope of its tangent line.

A gradient collects partial derivatives for a multivariable function. It points toward the direction of greatest local increase, so gradient descent uses the negative gradient.

Finite differences estimate a derivative by comparing nearby function values. The step must be chosen carefully: a very large step is biased, while a very small step can lose precision.
